Salmon with Brown Sugar Glaze

Home

Description

This Salmon with Brown Sugar Glaze is the perfect combination of sweet and savory. The glaze forms a light caramelized crust as it bakes, enhancing the natural richness of the salmon with a delicious depth of flavor. It’s simple, quick, and incredibly satisfying — ideal for both busy weeknights and special dinners

The recipe uses just a handful of common ingredients and requires minimal prep time. Whether you're new to cooking or just looking for an easy go-to fish dish, this recipe delivers impressive results with very little effort. Serve it with rice, roasted vegetables, or a light salad for a well-balanced and flavorful meal.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (skinless or skin-on, your choice)
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a small bowl, mix together the brown sugar, Dijon mustard, soy sauce, and olive oil until smooth.
  3. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease it. Place the salmon fillets on the sheet, skin-side down if using skin-on fillets
  4. Spoon or brush the brown sugar glaze evenly over the top of each salmon fillet.
  5. Sprinkle a little salt and pepper on top to taste.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.
  7. Remove from the oven and let rest for a minute or two. Serve warm with your favorite side dishes.
