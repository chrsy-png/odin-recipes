Pesto Cheesy Chicken Rolls

Home

Description

Pesto Cheesy Chicken Rolls are a flavorful and elegant twist on classic stuffed chicken. Each tender chicken breast is filled with creamy basil pesto and gooey melted cheese, then rolled up and baked to golden perfection. The rich, herby taste of pesto pairs beautifully with the juicy chicken and the warm, melty interior, making each bite bursting with flavor. It’s a dish that looks fancy, but is surprisingly simple to prepare.

This meal is perfect for both casual family dinners and special occasions. It pairs wonderfully with pasta, roasted vegetables, or a fresh garden salad. Whether you're cooking for guests or just treating yourself to something delicious, Pesto Cheesy chicken Rolls offer comfort, taste, and presentation all in one easy recipe.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup basil pesto (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese (or slices of mozzarella)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Toothpicks or kitchen twine (to secure the rolls)
  • Optional: grated Parmesan for topping

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a baking dish or line it with parchment paper.
  2. Place the chicken breasts between two pieces of plastic wrap and gently pound them with a meat mallet or rolling pin until about 1/2 inch thick.
  3. Season both sides of each chicken breast with salt and pepper.
  4. Spread about 1–2 tablespoons of pesto over one side of each flattened chicken breast.
  5. Sprinkle or place mozzarella cheese over the pesto layer.
  6. Carefully roll each chicken breast up tightly and secure with toothpicks or tie with kitchen twine.
  7. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear the chicken rolls for 2–3 minutes on each side until lightly golden.
  8. Transfer the rolls to the prepared baking dish and bake for 20–25 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked (internal temp of 165°F / 74°C).
  9. Let rest for a few minutes before serving. Optionally, sprinkle with Parmesan or drizzle with extra pesto before serving.
