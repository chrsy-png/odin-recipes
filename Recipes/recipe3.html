Yummy Pork Chops

Description

Yummy Pork Chops are a classic comfort food that’s easy to prepare and always satisfying. These chops are pan-seared to a beautiful golden brown, then simmered in a savory, slightly sweet sauce that locks in moisture and flavor. The result is tender, juicy pork with a delicious glaze that pairs perfectly with mashed potatoes, rice, or roasted vegetables.

This recipe uses simple ingredients but delivers big taste. It’s ideal for a quick weeknight meal, yet tasty enough to serve to guests. Whether you use bone-in or boneless chops, this dish is a reliable go-to when you want something hearty and delicious without a lot of fuss.

Ingredients

  • 4 pork chops (bone-in or boneless)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch (optional, for thickening)

Steps

  1. Pat the pork chops dry with a paper towel. Season both sides with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once hot, add the pork chops and sear for 3–4 minutes per side, until golden brown.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together chicken broth, brown sugar, soy sauce, and Dijon mustard.
  4. Pour the sauce into the skillet with the pork chops. Reduce the heat to medium-low and cover the pan.
  5. Simmer for 10–15 minutes, or until the pork is fully cooked (internal temperature of 145°F / 63°C).
  6. For a thicker sauce, mix cornstarch with 1 tablespoon of water, then stir it into the pan and simmer for another 2 minutes.
  7. Remove from heat, let rest for a couple of minutes, and serve hot with your favorite sides.
